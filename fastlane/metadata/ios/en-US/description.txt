Block Games brings the most loved block puzzles together in one app.

• Block Puzzle: place pieces on an 8x8 or 10x10 board and blast full rows and columns.
• Block Sudoku: fill rows, columns and 3x3 boxes on a 9x9 board.

Every game has tens of thousands of levels, daily and weekly missions, endless, time attack and relaxing zen modes.
Plays offline. Works on phones, tablets and the web. No gambling or games of chance.
