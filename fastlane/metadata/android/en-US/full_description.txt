Block Games brings 17 different block puzzles and 20 board variants together in one app. Every game keeps its own progress, missions and achievements.

• Block Puzzle Master: Place pieces on the board and clear full rows and columns.
• Block Sudoku: Fill rows, columns and boxes on a 9x9 board.
• Block Fill: Fill the shape completely with the pieces given.
• Falling Blocks: Rotate falling pieces and complete rows.
• Hex Blocks: Complete lines in three directions on a hex board.
• Block Slide: Slide blocks to close the gaps.
• Number Drop: Merge equal numbers and reach big tiles.
• Hex Rain: Group falling hexagons and melt them.
• Retro Console: Race, Snake, Shoot and Bricks on one screen.
• Block Escape: Follow the arrows and clear the board in the right order.
• Five-Block Fit: Cover the board with five-square pieces.
• Color Doors: Send each block out through its own colored door.
• Sand Blocks: Build color bridges with blocks that turn into sand.
• Cube Master 3D: Complete the cube layer by layer.
• Balance Tower: Take turns pulling blocks with a rival without toppling the tower.
• Tilt Tray: Tilt the tray and melt matching colors together.
• Area Split: Split the board into rectangles by the numbers.
• Block Variety: 20 different boards and piece sets.

Highlights
• 50,000 to 100,000 levels per game, each checked by a solver.
• Daily and weekly levels, missions, achievements and collectibles.
• Hints and undo whenever you get stuck.
• Symbol mode for color blindness, 33 languages, right-to-left support.
• Plays offline. Works on phones, tablets and the web.
• No account, birth date or personal data needed.
• No gambling, games of chance or 18+ content.

"Remove ads" and "Premium" purchases apply to every game in the app.
