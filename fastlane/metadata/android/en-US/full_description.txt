Block Games brings 17 different block puzzles and 20 board variants together in one app. Every game keeps its own progress, missions and achievements.

• Block Puzzle Master: Place blocks, blast lines!
• Block Sudoku: Fill rows, columns and 3x3 boxes!
• Block Fill: Fill the shape with every piece!
• Falling Blocks: Place the pieces, clear the lines!
• Hex Blocks: Complete lines in three directions!
• Block Slide: Slide, drop, complete the row!
• Number Drop: Merge equal numbers, grow them!
• Hex Rain: Spin the hexagon, match the colors!
• Retro Console: 4 classics on a dot screen
• Block Escape: Find the order, clear them all!
• Five-Block Fit: Fill the rectangle with five-cell pieces!
• Color Doors: Every block through its own color door!
• Sand Blocks: Pour the sand, build a color bridge!
• Cube Master 3D: Turn the pieces, complete the cube!
• Balance Tower: Pull, stack, and let your rival topple it!
• Tilt Tray: Tilt, match, melt!
• Area Split: Split by the numbers, fill the board!
• Block Variety: boards and piece sets

Highlights
• 50,000 to 100,000 levels per game, each checked by a solver.
• Hints and undo whenever you need them.
• Daily and weekly levels, missions, achievements and 33 languages.
• Plays offline on phones, tablets and the web.
• No gambling, betting or 18+ content; suitable for all ages.

Purchases (such as removing ads) apply to every game in the app.
